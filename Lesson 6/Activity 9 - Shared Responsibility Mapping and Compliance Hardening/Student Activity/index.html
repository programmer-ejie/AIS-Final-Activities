ACTIVITY 9 ? STUDENT GUIDE

Start your security audit

Follow these steps to inspect the simulated environment, complete the missing security controls, and document your results.

01

Inspect the starter

Open js/config/cloud-config.js for the simulated configuration and js/services/audit-service.js for the control checks and audit engine. Select Run audit to see the starter results.

02

Complete the missing checks

Follow TODO 1 in js/config/cloud-config.js and TODO 2 in js/services/audit-service.js: add MFA and disclosure policy settings, then implement their validation functions. Set MFA to fail and disclosure to pass initially.

03

Capture the initial audit

Run the audit again and capture a screenshot of all six checks, including the failing controls.

04

Remediate and verify

Update the failing settings in cloudConfig, save your changes, refresh the page, and run another audit. Verify six passes and capture the final results.

05

Document and submit

  • Complete the shared responsibility matrix and prepare a PDF audit report.
  • For each of the six controls, include a screenshot of its assessment and explain the result.
  • For each failed control, explain why it failed, who is responsible, what you changed, and how the correction resolves it.
  • Include before-and-after screenshots and show the final passing audit.
  • Submit the PDF, completed source code, repository link, screenshots, and responsibility matrix through your LMS.
Simulation only. No real cloud infrastructure is modified. Refreshing the page resets the configuration and audit history.
